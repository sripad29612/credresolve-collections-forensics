⚠ Executive Causal Audit Finding
"An apparent 11% recovery improvement does not translate into an 11% improvement in underlying daily collection performance."
●
Calendar Illusion: March's ~11.0% gross increase (+₹1.86 Cr / +₹1,86,29,129) was driven by March having 31 days vs February having 28 days (+10.7% more days). Daily gross run-rate grew by only +0.2%.
●
Reversal Leakage: Payment reversals totaled ₹9,46,58,651 (1,253 dishonored receipts), reducing gross receipts from ₹131.47 Cr down to ₹1,22,00,34,814 in true net cash recovery.
●
Attribution vs. Impact: WhatsApp captures ₹32,48,92,114 in 48h last-touch attributed recovery, but attribution does not prove causal incremental impact or ROI. Test via controlled experiment before scaling.
●
Truncated Aug Data: August 2026 contains only 8 days of data (₹4,41,50,645 collected at steady ₹55,18,831/day pace). Must not be reported as a monthly collapse.

Causal Dissection
📅

1. The Calendar Day Illusion

Gross collections jumped +10.96% between February (₹17.00 Cr / ₹17,00,05,293) and March (₹18.86 Cr / ₹18,86,34,421). However, February had 28 days and March had 31 days (+10.71% more operating days).

Normalized Daily Gross Change: +0.20% (Flat)
📉

2. Post-March Performance Volatility

If targeting optimization had created a sustained 11% monthly lift, collections would have compounded. Instead, April collapsed by -7.21% and June dropped -5.34%.

April Net MoM Contraction: -₹1.26 Cr (-₹1,25,58,086 / -7.21%)
📊

3. Cumulative Ratio Conflation

By July 2026, cumulative net collections reached ₹1,17.59 Cr (₹1,17,58,84,169), which equals 11.21% of the ₹1,048.90 Cr (₹10,48,90,35,343) portfolio. Internal presentations confused an 11% cumulative ratio with a monthly velocity improvement.

July Cumulative Net Ratio: 11.21% of Book

🔬 Recommended Controlled Experiment (RCT) Protocol

Before authorizing capital deployment for channel expansions or marketing tools, CredResolve must test WhatsApp payment-link enhancements through a pre-registered Randomized Controlled Trial.

Arm A: WhatsApp Dynamic Link (Treatment)

N = 7,500 Accounts. Debtors receive instant WhatsApp interactive templates with pre-filled UPI deep-links, waiver calculators, and instant receipt generation.

Arm B: SMS / Voice Standard (Control 1)

N = 7,500 Accounts. Standard plain-text SMS notifications and IVR dialer prompts directing debtors to a generic portal.

Arm C: Multi-Touch Hybrid (Treatment 2)

N = 7,500 Accounts. Voice bot call triggering an immediate follow-up WhatsApp link during live conversation.

Arm D: Legacy Baseline (Pure Control)

N = 7,500 Accounts. Business-as-usual collections cadence without automated digital deep links.

🎯

Primary Evaluation Endpoint

30-Day Net Realized Recovery Rate (%), strictly deducting all payment reversals and dishonored NACH mandates.

⚖

Statistical Power & Guardrails

Sample size of 7,500 per arm provides 95% statistical power to detect a true $\pm 1.25\%$ difference at $\alpha = 0.05$. No manual agent cross-contamination permitted.

🛑

Governance Warning on ₹10 Crore Investment

Do not manufacture an unverified financial return for the proposed ₹10 crore capital outlay. Capital must only be committed if Arm A demonstrates statistically significant causal lift.

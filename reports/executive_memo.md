### 2. Forensic Deconstruction: The Three Pillars of the 11% Illusion

Our forensic analysis uncovered three distinct mechanisms that produced the erroneous belief in sustained 11% growth:

```
[ Calendar Expansion: +10.7% Days ] ──┐
[ Payment Reversals: ₹9.47 Cr Leak ] ─┼─> Headline "11% Lift" Collapses to +0.2% Daily Gross Growth
[ Cumulative Ratio Conflation ] ─────┘
```

1. **The Calendar Day Illusion (March 2026):**
   Gross collections rose from ₹170.01M in February to ₹188.63M in March (+10.96%, approximately 11%). However, February had 28 calendar days while March had 31 (+10.71% more operating days). On a calendar-normalized basis, **daily gross collections grew by only +0.20%** (₹6.07M/day in Feb vs. ₹6.08M/day in Mar).
2. **Reversal Leakage & Post-March Volatility:**
   Dishonored instruments surged in March to ₹14.36M, compressing March net recovery growth to +9.94%. In subsequent months, net recovery fluctuated wildly without compounding: **-7.21% in April (-₹1.26 Cr)**, +6.03% in May, **-5.34% in June**, and +5.95% in July.
3. **Cumulative Ratio Conflation:**
   By the end of July 2026, cumulative net collections reached ₹1,175.88M, which represented **11.21% of the total ₹10.49B portfolio book**. Internal presentations erroneously conflated an 11% *cumulative portfolio milestone* with an 11% *monthly velocity improvement*.

### 4. Strategic Governance & Capital Allocation Guidance

CredResolve's proposed **₹10 Crore investment** in full-scale communications automation cannot be justified under current attribution models. Management must enforce the following pre-conditions:

1. **Mandatory Randomized Controlled Trial (RCT):**
   Partition 30,000 active delinquent accounts into four strictly isolated arms ($N = 7,500$ each):
   - **Arm A (Treatment):** Interactive WhatsApp messaging with dynamic instant-payment links.
   - **Arm B (Active Control):** Standard plain-text SMS notifications and IVR dialer prompts.
   - **Arm C (Hybrid):** Voice conversational AI with instant WhatsApp link dispatch.
   - **Arm D (Pure Control):** Standard collections protocol without automated link pushes.
2. **Pre-Registered Primary Endpoint:**
   Evaluate success strictly on **30-Day Net Realized Recovery Rate (%)**, deducting all payment reversals and dishonored instruments. Statistical power is 95% to detect a true $\pm1.25\%$ difference at $\alpha = 0.05$.
3. **Capital Release Trigger:**
   Release capital in phased tranches: commit ₹50 Lakhs for the 60-day trial; release the remaining ₹9.5 Crore **only if Arm A demonstrates a statistically significant ($p < 0.01$) lift of at least +2.5 percentage points** over Arm B after accounting for channel operating expenses.
